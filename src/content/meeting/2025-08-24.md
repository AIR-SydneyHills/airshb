## 10:00am: Liam Shorte from Sonas Wealth - Obtaining appropriate financial advice.

![](../../assets/images/liam_shorte_headshot-med.jpg "liam")

Liam is a very popular speaker at our branch and has
returned at the special request of our members. In this talk, Liam will provide
some useful guidance to ensure that retirees receive financial advice that
meets their needs and is suitable to their circumstances.

Retirees will gain some tips on how to prepare for meetings
with their financial advisor, so that the advisor understands their current
financial situation and their short and longer term financial goals and
challenges. Liam will also explain how the advice process works and what good
personal financial advice actually looks like. Liam also took our members through the process of finding a reputable advisor, plus all the  RED FLAGS that you need to look out for. ***Members can review this video on our Zoom site***
