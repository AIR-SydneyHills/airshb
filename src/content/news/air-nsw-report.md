Two NSW Branches, Sydney Hills and St George, shared a stand at the 2025 NSW Seniors Festival Expo held at the International Convention Centre, Sydney (ICC) on Wednesday and Thursday, March 12 & 13, 2025; it was a wonderful collaboration The atmosphere was buzzing. Thousands passed through the doors and hopefully we will get some new members. To entice attendees to our stall we had a raffle of various financial oriented books. There were plenty of pamphlets given away and some interesting discussions from those who came up to talk to us. This was our first year of doing this and the experience we gained will ensure that next year we will be even better.

It is anticipated that the new membership system (Membership Pro) rollout is expected to be completed by June 30th.
